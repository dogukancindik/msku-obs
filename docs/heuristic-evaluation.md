# Heuristic evaluation & design rationale — MSKÜ OBS redesign

Based on screenshots of the current MSKÜ OBS (November 2026). Severity: Nielsen 0–4. Use this as source material for the report; rewrite it in your own words.

| # | Area | Problem in current OBS | Redesign | Principles | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | shell | "Genel İşlemler" alone opens 15 links (Kullanım Kılavuzu, Özlük, Harç, Bölüm Ders Programı…), "Ders ve Dönem İşlemleri" another 10; related pages live in different groups (Sınav Takvimi in Genel, Not Listesi in Ders ve Dönem). | **Sidebar: ~30 items → 11 goal-based items (Hick’s law).** Four labelled groups — Genel, Akademik & Ders İşlemleri, Takvim & Katalog, İletişim & Hesap — with 11 destinations. Decision time grows with the number of choices (Hick–Hyman), so fewer, clearer choices are faster. _Moved/changed: Sınav Takvimi joined Ders Programı on one page; Mesajlar and Hesap moved to the bottom, next to Çıkış._ | Hick’s law / menu breadth 7±2 (Lec5); Grouping & Gestalt proximity (Lec5); Mental model match (Lec7) (Lec5 · Lec7) | 3 · Major |
| 2 | shell | "2026-2027 Güz" in the header is a bordered badge that looks clickable but does nothing (false affordance); each page has its own term dropdown. | **Term chip became a real selector.** One working term selector in the top bar, shared by all term-dependent pages. _Moved/changed: Fake badge → real <select>; per-page dropdowns removed._ | Affordances (Lec5); Consistency — Shneiderman #1; Reduce memory load — Shneiderman #8 (Lec5 · Lec6) | 2 · Minor |
| 3 | shell | Home icon duplicates the menu, a megaphone with a red "2" has no clear meaning, and the floating blue accessibility button covers table columns on every page. | **Top bar reduced to three things.** Only term, messages/notifications and profile. | 5 | login | Students must remember and type the full institutional address. | **Domain suffix shown, typing errors prevented.** The field shows "@posta.mu.edu.tr" as a suffix and completes it automatically; other domains get an inline, actionable error. | Error prevention (Shneiderman #5); Recognition rather than recall (Nielsen #6); Constraints (Norman) (Lec3 · Lec6) | 2 · Minor |

| 7 | dashboard | Home shows 8 static boxes (Öğrenci Kimliği, Süre Bilgisi, Ek Akademik Bilgiler "-1"…) that look like cards but cannot be clicked. | **Summary cards are actionable.** Four cards (AGNO, Aktif Dönem, Mezuniyet, Devamsızlık). On hover they lift and show ↗; click opens the page with the details. _Moved/changed: Passive boxes → buttons (whole card is the hit area)._ | False affordance → explicit affordance (Lec5); Gulf of execution (Norman, Lec6); Microinteractions (Lec5) (Lec5 · Lec6) | 3 · Major |
| 8 | dashboard | Programme name appears in 3 cards, "Fakülte" twice in one card, status "Aktif (E-Devletten Kayıt Yapan)" twice, and "-1" under Ek Akademik Bilgiler. | **Duplicate and meaningless data removed.** Each fact appears once. Identity details live in Hesap & Kullanıcı İşlemleri. | Aesthetic & minimalist design (Nielsen #8); 1D — meaningful words (Lec2) (Lec2 · Lec4) | 2 · Minor |
| 9 | dashboard | No context: students open Ders Programı or Sınav Takvimi to know what is next. | **Today’s classes & upcoming exams.** The system takes the initiative: next classes and the next exams with a countdown, each linking to the full view. | Dialog initiative (Dix, Lec6); Recognition over recall (Lec7) (Lec6 · Lec7) | 2 · Minor |

| 11 | courses | A red alarm banner says registration is closed, while the page also says "Kesinleştirildi" and "Onaylandı" in small text; the footer shows a red "UYARI! | 12 | courses | Column headers "Z/S", "T+U", "Alış: Alttan", "Aç.Nedeni: Dnm." need insider knowledge. | **Cryptic columns explained.** Plain chips (Zorunlu / Seçmeli), "T+U" with a tooltip, abbreviations removed. | 1D — words (Lec2); Match between system and real world (Nielsen #2) (Lec2) | 2 · Minor |
| 13 | grades | Every course gets its own table with the same 5-column header; most contain "Kayıt Yok" or "--". | **One table instead of a table per course.** One row per course: Vize, Ödev/Proje, Final, class average, status. | 15 | grades | "Not" and "Sınıf Ort." are separated by the "İlan Tarihi" column and left-aligned. | **Numbers right-aligned, class average next to the score.** Tabular, right-aligned numbers; class average and difference shown under each score. | Alignment of numbers (Lec4); Scanning across gaps is hard (Lec4) (Lec4) | 1 · Cosmetic |

| 17 | transcript | Dense two-column scenario table, an edit icon per row, a separate "prepare" step and a toast that repeats inside the page. | **What-if simulator with live feedback.** Change a letter in a dropdown or add an elective → new AGNO, YANO and the difference (+ green / − red) update instantly. | 19 | curriculum | A long unordered list of course codes mixed across years. | **Curriculum by semester with status filter.** Semester tabs 1–8 and a status filter (Tümü / Tamamlandı / Alınıyor / Alınmadı). | Flexibility (Dix); Selective attention (Lec7) (Lec6 · Lec7) | 2 · Minor |
| 20 | averages | Two bar charts with a different colour per bar (colour carries no meaning); "Muaf" is plotted as a term at 4.00 and the current term as 0.00, which distorts the scale. | **One honest chart instead of two rainbow charts.** One chart: YANO as bars, AGNO as a line, consistent colours, hover tooltips; the in-progress term is marked, not plotted as zero. | Don’t overdo colour (Lec4); Expectations & meanings (Lec4); Data honesty (Lec4) | 2 · Minor |

| 22 | schedule | The same course is repeated in 4 separate hourly cells (e.g. SE 4009 08:30, 09:30, 10:30, 11:30), each repeating the name, room and instructor; an empty 06:00–06:45 row. | **Consecutive hours merged into one block.** One block per session (08:30 – 12:15) with Teori/Uygulama label; grid starts at 08:30. | Gestalt: continuity & common region (Lec7); Reduce visual noise (Lec5) (Lec5 · Lec7) | 3 · Major |
| 23 | schedule | "Detaylı / Sade / Yazdır" are tiny unlabeled text buttons in the corner. | **Detailed / simple view and print as clear controls.** A segmented toggle and a labelled "Programı Yazdır" button with a print-optimised layout. | Flexibility & customizability (Dix); Explicit affordances (Lec5) (Lec5 · Lec6) | 1 · Cosmetic |

| 25 | offered | One long unsorted table; columns "T", "U/L", "Z" with ✓ icons; the same course listed once per section. | **Searchable, filterable course catalogue.** Search box, year filter (1–4) and type filter; sections grouped into one row. | Flexibility (Dix); Recognition over recall (Nielsen #6); 1D — words (Lec2) (Lec2 · Lec6) | 2 · Minor |
